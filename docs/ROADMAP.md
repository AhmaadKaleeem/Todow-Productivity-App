# Future Roadmap

Todow supports student schedules today and can also organize work and personal tasks. This roadmap adds integrations while keeping core planning local-first.

Phase 1 focuses on Google accounts. The app will handle multiple accounts and turn Gmail messages into tasks. It will also sync with Google Calendar so events and tasks live in the same place.

Phase 2 brings in academic tools. The app will import assignments from Google Classroom and let you attach Google Drive files directly to tasks. It will also pull in university timetable data.

Phase 3 is about sync and backup. The app will sync across multiple devices and run automatic backups. You will still be able to make offline changes.

Phase 4 improves the focus sessions. I want to add Android app blocking and let you configure which apps are allowed while studying. It will also track your focus history.

Phase 5 tackles advanced planning. This covers recurring tasks, weekly and monthly planning, and goal tracking. I also plan to add milestone dependencies and more flexible reminder rules.

Phase 6 adds profession and work categories. Users can choose a built-in category or enter a custom profession or work type. A shared cloud catalog can save new entries and offer them to other users as selectable categories.

The long-term goal is one place for tasks, deadlines, schedules, files, focus sessions, and planning for study, work, and personal projects.
